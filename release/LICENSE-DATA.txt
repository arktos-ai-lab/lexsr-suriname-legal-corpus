LexSR corpus v1.0: licence

The texts in this corpus are Surinamese legislation, regulations and other general
rules issued by public authority, and decisions of the Hof van Justitie and other courts
of Suriname. Article 11 of the Auteurswet 1913 (G.B. 1913 no. 15, as amended) provides
that no copyright subsists in laws, decrees and ordinances issued by public authority,
nor in judicial decisions and administrative decisions. These texts are therefore free
of copyright in Suriname and are redistributed here without restriction.

The texts of 21 works that do not fall under Article 11 (student theses, standards-body
documents, third-party technical codes, a policy plan) are withheld: their rows,
identifiers and citations remain, but the text fields are empty. The list and reasons
are in the works table (metadata field "text_withheld").
